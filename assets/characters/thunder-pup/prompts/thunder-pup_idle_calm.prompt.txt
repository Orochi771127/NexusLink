## Core Silhouette

- Small four-legged wolf pup, cute in proportion but not round, babyish, or toy-like.
- Head is slightly large relative to the body, clearly reading as pup-stage.
- Ears are large, upright, and sharply triangular with a clear pointed-ear wolf silhouette.
- Face shape must read as wolf pup, not cat, fox, rabbit, or generic dog.
- Chest has a fluffy fur tuft, visible in front view and readable as soft inner chest fur.
- Legs are short, stable, and sturdy; paws are thick and grounded with visible toe blocks / paw mass.
- Body is compact and agile, not long adult-wolf anatomy and not chubby super-deformed.
- Tail base is a fluffy wolf tail; the rear half transitions into a large lightning crystal cluster.
- Back and rear neck include a small amount of spiky fur / lightning-energy tuft language, but not armor.
- Overall silhouette must stay readable at small companion size.

## Face / Eye Identity

- Eyes are large, bright, glowing electric cyan-blue.
- Eye shape is slightly sharp and intelligent, but still pup-like; never adult-wolf vicious.
- Brows / brow markings create alert, clever, quick-reacting focus.
- Nose is small and black.
- Muzzle area is light gray / silver-gray and must remain wolf-pup shaped.
- Cheek and lower-face pale markings frame the muzzle without turning into a fox mask.
- Front expression is calm, alert, approachable, and smart, not silly.
- Do not over-smile, over-cute, or convert the face into an exaggerated chibi mascot.

## Color System

- Primary fur: charcoal black / near-black.
- Secondary fur: dark cool gray.
- Muzzle and inner chest: light gray / silver gray.
- Primary glow: electric blue.
- Secondary glow: violet / blue-violet.
- Crystal highlights: lavender / pale violet / cyan highlight.
- Palette must remain black / gray / electric-blue / blue-violet.
- Do not drift into white wolf, mint green, gold, warm fire, leafy green, or ice-only palettes.

## Lightning Markings

- Forehead center has one primary lightning crest extending upward from the forehead, with electric blue core and violet-blue glow.
- Chest has a V-shaped / Y-shaped cyan-blue glowing mark, framed by the silver-gray chest tuft.
- Outer cheeks and behind the ears carry blue-violet glowing fur tufts / lightning-energy texture.
- Body sides carry irregular branching lightning marks, especially visible in the side standing reference.
- Front legs and rear legs each have short glowing lightning marks.
- Markings should read as glowing energy cracks / electric current paths, not printed decals, spots, stickers, or armor trim.
- Lightning marks should flow along the body and limb structure.
- Do not turn the markings into symmetrical decorative armor patterns.
- Do not let markings jump to unrelated positions between frames.

## Tail Crystal

- Tail base remains a fluffy wolf tail.
- Rear half of the tail is fused with a lightning crystal structure.
- The crystal is a multi-piece clustered form, spreading outward into several sharp shard tips.
- Crystal color runs from violet / lavender to electric blue, with cyan highlights.
- Internal crystal lines contain electric-blue glowing marks.
- The tail crystal cluster is one of ThunderPup's strongest identity markers.
- It must not be weakened, hidden, made tiny, or converted into a normal fluffy tail.
- It must not become a fire tail, mechanical tail, metal tail, leaf tail, or generic energy plume.
- In side and movement animations, the crystal cluster should angle back and slightly upward, staying attached to the fluffy base.

## Material Language

- Fur is soft, dark, and painterly with clear tufts, not plastic and not pure machine surface.
- Silver-gray muzzle and chest fur should feel soft and layered.
- Lightning marks are glow embedded in fur / energy cracks, not hard armor plates.
- Tail crystal should feel crystalline and energetic, not metal armor.
- Black-iron / cyber thunder language should be restrained, sharp, and electric rather than fantasy heart-council, nature spirit, or heavy mech.

## Personality Read From Design

- Core temperament: alert, loyal, agile, with a small wild edge.
- Emotional baseline: calm observation first, rapid reaction second.
- Best acting direction: guarded curiosity / alert affection.
- Protective but not aggressive.
- Calm when trusted.
- Defensive when boundary pressure rises.
- Do not perform as an overly naive baby.
- Do not perform as a violent villain.
- Do not perform as a proud adult king / alpha-wolf ruler.
- Do not perform as a soft rabbit-like healer mascot.
