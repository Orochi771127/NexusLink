# Linkara Six-Region Diorama R2 Prompts

All day prompts were image edits of the matching R1 day candidate. All night prompts were lighting-only edits of the resulting R2 day master.

## Shared day constraints

Preserve the exact vertical camera, region identity, landmark order, approach path and empty circular or octagonal companion plaza. Rebuild the scene as a handcrafted 3D miniature diorama photographed with a fixed orthographic-like camera: rounded polymer-clay forms, hand-painted resin, subtly imperfect model-kit edges, translucent resin water/crystals, controlled soft specular highlights, ambient occlusion between physical pieces, shallow scale-model depth of field only outside the gameplay plaza, and atmospheric haze over the far distance. Keep the foreground plaza crisp, broad and empty. No characters, creatures, people, text, UI, logo, frame, rain, weather streaks, sun, moon or stars. Mobile portrait 9:16.

## Region day direction

- `central_radiant_core`: ivory clay towers, gold-painted trim, turquoise clear-resin canals, pearl and pale-blue model landscaping; grand but tactile, not photoreal architecture.
- `eastern_mystic_mountains`: charcoal and violet clay rock modules, translucent amethyst resin crystals, restrained purple internal glow, pale mist separating distant ridges.
- `northern_verdant_plains`: rounded plasticine trees and hedges, hand-built cottage miniatures, wheat-colored clay fields, glossy cyan resin river, cheerful tabletop-model warmth.
- `southeast_forge_hills`: soot-black clay foundry modules, copper-painted pipes, orange lava resin and cobalt energy resin, visible assembled model-kit construction without cluttering the central platform.
- `southern_harbor_nexus`: ivory clay harbor city, blue-painted domes and awnings, miniature ships and rope details, clear turquoise resin water, bright collectible-diorama finish.
- `southwest_tidal_frontier`: dark blue-black clay cliffs, timber-like resin piers, violet translucent seams and crystals, glossy storm-dark ocean resin, dangerous but readable atmosphere with no baked rain.

## Shared night relight

Use the matching R2 day master as the sole composition source. Keep camera, geometry, silhouette, plaza size, stairs, roads, docks, waterline and every major landmark fixed. Change lighting only: deep blue night ambience, warm practical miniature lamps, restrained region-color resin glow, softened far-distance visibility and readable foreground plaza. Do not add or remove objects. No moon, sun, stars, rain, weather streaks, characters, creatures, text, UI, logo or frame.
