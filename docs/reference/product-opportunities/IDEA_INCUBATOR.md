## High-Risk Research Only

These ideas require separate governance and must not be treated as ordinary feature candidates:

- sleep or health interpretation
- financial behavior analysis
- relationship coaching
- parenting support
- elder-care support
- crisis detection
- medical or mental-health screening
- continuous location or microphone context
- passive reading of messages, email, or private files

## Rejected by Default

- streaks and missed-day punishment
- affection meters used to coerce return
- companion distress caused by user absence
- loot-box or gacha utility rewards
- universal life scoring
- public ranking of emotional progress
- automated diagnosis
- investment, legal, or medication recommendations
- engagement notifications framed as emotional obligation
- background surveillance without a narrow necessity

## Promotion Checklist

An idea may leave this incubator only when it has:

1. a named target user and recurring job;
2. a clear companion advantage;
3. Canon conflict analysis;
4. privacy and safety analysis;
5. a minimal reversible experiment;
6. success and kill criteria;
7. explicit owner approval for the next state.
