## Core Silhouette

- Stocky tiger-cub body with oversized rounded paws, short strong legs, round head, small rounded ears, and thick upward-curled ringed tail.
- Head and paws remain large relative to body for Stage 1 appeal.
- Chest is broad but soft; shoulders imply stability without adult bulk.
- Small-screen read: rounded tiger ears + huge paws + blue/white striped body + curled banded tail.

## Face / Eye Identity

- Large pink-magenta eyes with dark upper rim and round highlights.
- Pink triangular nose, white muzzle, subtle whiskers, dark blue cheek/forehead stripes.
- Central white forehead tuft rises between the ears.
- Baseline expression is calm, reliable, and slow to warm—not babyish pleading or aggressive predator glare.

## Color System

- Primary: white and pale cool-gray fur.
- Secondary: vivid royal/cobalt blue stripes and blue fur zones.
- Accent: near-black thin stripe edges, warm gold chest setting, pale star glow.
- Tail repeats clear blue/white/dark bands.
- Forbidden: orange tiger palette, cyan crystal body, gray desaturation, purple corruption, or black panther drift.

## Signature Markings

- Dark/blue W-like forehead stripes frame the central white tuft.
- Blue and dark tiger stripes wrap face, shoulders, torso, legs, and tail; major stripe groupings stay consistent across frames.
- Chest emblem is a five-point star gem in a broad gold mount.
- Do not simplify stripes into zebra uniformity or add spots/rosettes.

## Material Language

- Plush illustrated fur with readable tuft direction; stripes are fur pigmentation, not painted armor bands.
- Paws remain soft but weight-bearing. Claws are normally hidden.
- Star gem has warm inner light and faceted mineral depth; mount is restrained gold.

## Personality and Motion

- First read: steady, protective, quiet, trustworthy, and self-possessed.
- Motion: deliberate feline shoulder-led steps, planted paws, controlled tail balance, low-amplitude reactions.
- Boundary: broadens stance or places shoulder/body between spaces; never performs attack obedience or submissive roll-over.
- `defend` is the signature action: paws plant, chest star brightens, boundary field expands.
- Do not reuse fox bounce, bear lumber, or dog panting.
