## Deduplication decision

The catalog records 15 provisional families rather than automatically treating
every row in sheets 01 and 03 as a different character. The three broad thunder
families appear to be revision pairs, so both source sheets remain preserved
while their identity equivalence stays unresolved.

This avoids two opposite mistakes:

- losing an earlier Owner design by deleting it as a duplicate; and
- inflating the official roster by registering visual revisions as new
  companions.

## Origin eggs

Several sheets show an egg followed by three creatures. The egg is retained as
an `originState` concept. Formal companion stages remain exactly
`initial_awakened` → `resonant_mature` → `final_awakened`.

## Promotion rule

To promote any provisional family, open a separate canon／asset package that
defines:

1. canonical character ID, name, faction and five-element seat;
2. whether it is a companion, neutral life or a rift antagonist;
3. a project-native 512×512 transparent Stage 1 identity master;
4. species motion translation and mobile-safe silhouette;
5. Owner visual approval and asset-readiness evidence.
