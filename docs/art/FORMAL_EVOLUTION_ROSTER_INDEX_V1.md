## Blazetail reference decision

- Owner image 1 is Stage 2 `resonant_mature`.
- Owner image 2 is Stage 3 `final_awakened`.
- The pixel images are design-reference authority for clothing, color and flame
  motifs. They are not runtime masters and will be translated to the existing
  high-resolution illustrated companion style.
- Stage 2 keeps the warm red-gold guardian robe and yellow-orange flame.
- Stage 3 keeps the dark Cyber-Taoist robe, cyan eyes and cyan／magenta
  resonance flame.
- Both retain one tail, fox anatomy, the orange face and cream muzzle identity
  and a cute readable expression. In accordance with the Owner references,
  both Stage 2 and Stage 3 are upright anthropomorphic fox forms; neither
  becomes an unrelated human or a nine-tailed fox.

## Current package boundary

The table is a naming and art-review index. All current design-board links point
to R2. It does not make Black Iron later
forms live, does not advance a save, and does not alter current Codex or Growth
runtime data. The five proposed Black Iron names become current canon only
after Owner review.
